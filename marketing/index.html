The problem

School asked one question. It was the wrong one.

For most of us, school asked: can you reproduce the answer the examiner wants? If you could, you were “bright”. If you couldn’t, you were told, in a hundred quiet ways, that you weren’t.

But you know people who failed exams and built businesses. People who never finished school and can read a room, fix any engine or solve a problem no one else could see. You may be one of them.

The belief

Intelligence is not fixed. It is waiting to be activated.

HiLabs is built on the Human Intelligence Models (HIMs) framework. It starts from a simple idea: everyone carries capacity that only shows up when the right environment brings it out.

The system just wasn’t built to look for it. So we don’t ask “How smart are you?”

We ask: “Where do you go deeper, and what happens when you’re given the room to?”

Capacity is hidden

It shows up in how you engage, not in a test score.

Environment decides

The right people and the right challenge bring it out.

Work is the evidence

What you make shows what you can do, better than a grade ever could.

How it works

Four steps. One loop. Built around you.

Most courses hand you a syllabus and a deadline. HiLabs starts with you, then builds the path as it learns what pulls you in.

01

Explore

Start with a question, not a course

Bring something you want to understand. Try short challenges in logic, design, people, data, fast learning and quick decisions. Go deeper wherever you want to. Nothing is graded and nothing is forced.

02

Learn

Learn the way apprentices do

Spend time around people who do the work well. Watch how experts think out loud, study their real cases, and pick up their judgment before anyone lectures you.

03

Develop

Practise exactly what you need

Get targeted exercises on the gaps in your own work, in the style that fits how you engage. An AI companion asks before it tells, and never does the work for you.

04

Demonstrate

Leave with proof, not a promise

Every draft, revision and project goes into your portfolio. Show people what you can actually do.

What you make shapes what comes next.

That loop is the whole idea.

Questions

Before you decide

Do I need a degree or good grades?
No. We never ask for them. All you need is curiosity and about 4 hours a week.
Is this a school? Will I get a certificate?
HiLabs isn’t a school, and the first cohort doesn’t award certificates. You leave with something more useful: a portfolio of dated work that shows how you think. It complements formal qualifications rather than replacing them.
Is it really free?
Yes, for the first cohort. You’re helping us test the approach, so we don’t charge you for it.
What is HIMs?
Human Intelligence Models is the framework behind HiLabs. It builds on decades of research by Howard Gardner, Lev Vygotsky, Carol Dweck and others. It sees intelligence as hidden capacity, shaped by environment and shown through what you make. Some of its ideas are well established; others are new and still being tested. HiLabs is where we test them.
Will the AI just give me the answers?
No. It’s designed to ask before it tells and to help you reason. It never writes your work for you.
Will you tell me what kind of intelligence I have?
Not as a label. At the end of your journey, a practitioner talks you through what you did: where you went deeper, where you kept going, and what you might explore next. Every observation links to your own work, and you can disagree with any of it.
What happens to my data?
We collect only what the study needs. We never sell it. You can download or delete everything at any time.
What comes after critical thinking?
Mathematics and language are next.
